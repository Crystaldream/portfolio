# About Me

I'm a passionate software developer who loves building elegant solutions to complex problems.

## What I Do

I specialize in:

- **Backend Development** - Building robust, scalable systems with Rust and modern frameworks
- **Full-Stack Web** - Creating seamless experiences from database to UI
- **System Design** - Architecting solutions that balance performance, maintainability, and simplicity

## My Approach

I believe in writing code that is not just functional, but maintainable and elegant. Every project is an opportunity to learn something new and push the boundaries of what's possible.

### Core Principles

1. **Clean Code** - Readable, well-documented, and tested
2. **Performance** - Fast by default, optimized where it matters
3. **User-Centric** - Technology should serve people, not the other way around
4. **Continuous Learning** - Always exploring new tools and techniques
